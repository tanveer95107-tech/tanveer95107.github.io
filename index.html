Frequently asked questions

How can I place an order? +

Add products to your cart, then tap "Order on WhatsApp" — your cart details will be pre-filled in a WhatsApp message. We'll confirm your order and delivery details from there.

Do you offer Men, Women and Kids collections? +

Yes! Tanu Store offers 75+ premium branded products across Men, Women and Kids categories. Explore each section from the navigation menu.

How can I contact Tanu Store? +

You can reach us on WhatsApp at +92 333 9917323 or follow @tanver_aly on Instagram. We reply quickly and are happy to help.

Can I ask about sizes before ordering? +

Absolutely. Message us on WhatsApp and we'll share available sizes, measurements, and product details so you can order with confidence.

Do you deliver across Pakistan? +

Yes — we deliver nationwide. Free shipping on orders above Rs. 5,000. Standard charges may apply for smaller orders depending on your location.

What is your return policy? +

If you receive a damaged or incorrect item, contact us within 24 hours of delivery and we'll resolve it promptly with a replacement or refund.
