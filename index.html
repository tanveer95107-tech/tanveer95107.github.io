Frequently Asked Questions

How can I order a product?

Select a product and add it to your cart. You can then contact Tanu Store through WhatsApp to confirm your order and delivery details. We'll guide you through the entire process.

Do you have Men, Women and Kids clothing?

Yes! Tanu Store offers premium branded collections for Men, Women and Kids. Browse our categories to explore all options. We have over 75+ products across all categories.

How can I contact Tanu Store?

You can contact us through WhatsApp at +92 333 9917323 or Instagram @tanver_aly using the links provided in the contact section below. We respond quickly!

Can I ask about sizes before ordering?

Absolutely! Contact us through WhatsApp and ask about available sizes, measurements, and product details before placing your order. We're happy to help you find the perfect fit.

Yes, we deliver across Pakistan. Delivery charges may apply based on your location. Free shipping on orders above Rs. 5,000. Contact us on WhatsApp for more details.

If you receive a damaged or incorrect product, please contact us within 24 hours of delivery. We'll resolve the issue promptly with a replacement or refund.
